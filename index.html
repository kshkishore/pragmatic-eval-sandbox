Pragmatic Evaluation Sandbox

Check new data against the rules and results of the thesis Agentic Evaluation of Pragmatic Competence in Multimodal Large Language Models, without touching the thesis data.

New test items

A file of items with a prompt, specification, reference and A, B and C responses. The sandbox checks the thesis's construction rules and compares the items with the 160 thesis items.

Template (JSON)

Chatbot replies

Replies of any chatbot to the 160 thesis prompts. The sandbox applies the format rule, the chart-fact rule and the text-type features, computes the metrics, and compares the results with G1 and G2.

Template (CSV)

Judge scores

A new judge's scores for the 480 A, B and C responses. The sandbox computes pairwise accuracy, agreement with J1 and J2 and McNemar tests, and compares the judge with every thesis judge.

    Everything runs in your browser. Your files are not uploaded anywhere and nothing is saved to the repository. The first check downloads Python and its packages (about 40 MB, cached afterwards) and takes about a minute to start. Judge files take longer to check, because the confidence intervals use 10,000 bootstrap resamples.

    How the checks are made
    
  
      
    
    • The rules are the thesis's own: the format rule, the chart-fact rule, the text-type features and the definition of pairwise accuracy, copied unchanged from the thesis code.
      • 
    
    • The checker compares new data with a frozen, read-only copy of the thesis data. Every check first verifies that copy against its checksums.
      • 
    
    • A self-test runs the thesis's own data through the checker and reproduces the published thesis numbers.
      • 
    
    • Only the lightweight metrics (BLEU, chrF, ROUGE-L, length) are computed here. The neural metrics and LLM judges need a local computer; see the thesis repository.
      • 
  
    
  
    You can also check data on GitHub: upload a file to submissions/<new-folder>/ in the repository, and the report appears in the Actions tab.
